About

Built on the road. Grounded in the trades.

I'm a Ballarat-based driver and tradesman with a track record of showing up, following procedure, and getting the job done safely — whether that's a Super B-double crossing three time zones or a house slab poured on schedule.

Since August 2025 I've driven interstate linehaul for Angad Transport out of Melbourne, holding an unrestricted MC licence and Basic Fatigue Management accreditation with zero demerits and zero infringements. Before that — and alongside it — I've spent years in concreting and bricklaying, including site supervision, where the same qualities apply: attention to detail, physical resilience, and accountability to a team.

I'm now applying that same discipline to a new goal: a long-term career as a Correctional Officer with the Tasmania Prison Service, and I'm ready to relocate to Hobart, Risdon, or Launceston to make it happen.
