The story

I didn’t arrive in tech by the obvious route.

The short version: I came to Australia, studied Computer Science, worked demanding jobs outside tech, and learned that the same mindset keeps showing up everywhere — understand the problem, stay calm, work it out, finish properly.

01
Foundation

India to Australia

Moving countries forced independence early. Australia became the place where study, work, money, responsibility and long-term plans all had to coexist at once.

02
Work

Construction taught me more than construction.

Formwork, concrete, plans, tools, crews and deadlines taught me to be useful, precise and accountable. There’s nowhere to hide when the physical result is right in front of you.

03
Perspective

The road made the world feel bigger.

Interstate transport took me across huge parts of Australia. It demanded patience, planning and self-management — but it was work I did, not the identity I wanted to build my future around.

04
Direction

Software is where the pieces connect.

Computer Science gives me the part I kept looking for: complex problems, systems that can be understood, and the ability to build something from an idea and make it genuinely useful.
